An imagined journey through Australia's Great Barrier Reef, not a dive map or a reconstruction of one reef. The depths are scene settings, not navigation advice.

Drag the water to look all the way around. Arrow keys look around too. Choose a habitat below, or swipe within its navigation strip. Tap a creature, or follow the encounter beneath each scene. Escape closes these notes.

The reef is living Sea Country, with connections held by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Traditional Owners.

Beauty is not a clean bill of health. Warming seas, bleaching and other pressures threaten the Reef. This world imagines thriving habitats; it does not report current reef condition.

Ecology: Reef biodiversity, coral spawning, herbivorous fish. Original procedural artwork. No tracking. Sound starts only when you ask.
